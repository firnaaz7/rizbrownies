Our Story

Born from a
passion
for chocolate

Riz Brownies started in a home kitchen with one simple obsession: the perfect brownie. No cakey texture, no dry edges — just deeply fudgy, crinkle-topped squares of pure happiness.

Every recipe has been tested dozens of times. We use only the finest Belgian chocolate, hand-harvested sea salt, and free-range eggs. Because you deserve the real thing.
